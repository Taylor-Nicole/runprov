# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume they are acting in good faith. Criticise the work, never the
person doing it.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages, code review and any other space
this project uses, and to private contact where it concerns the project.

## What is expected

* **Argue about the artifact.** "This record is wrong when the input moves" is a review.
  "You clearly do not understand provenance" is not, and it is also less useful.
* **Bring the evidence.** This project's whole argument is that a claim without a
  measurement behind it is a guess. That standard applies to claims about code and to
  claims about people.
* **Assume the other person knows something you do not.** Most disagreements here will be
  between people with different pipelines, different filesystems and different failure
  histories. Ask what broke before explaining why it cannot have.
* **Accept a "no" without escalating.** A maintainer declining a feature is a decision
  about scope, not a judgement about you.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments. Discrimination or exclusion on the basis of
age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level
of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity or orientation. Sexual attention or advances of any kind. Publishing
anyone's private information without their explicit permission. Sustained disruption of
discussion.

## Reporting

Report to **taylor.nicole.thompson@gmail.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept
confidential; you will get an acknowledgement within about a week.

If your report concerns the maintainer, or you would rather not write to them, raise it
with the affiliated institution (AP-HP, Hôpital Henri-Mondor) through its own channels.
That route exists so that "the only person you can report to is the person you are
reporting" is never the situation.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, code and issues that violate this
document, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they judge
inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. Where a response is not obvious, the
principle is: the smallest action that stops the harm and lets everyone else get back to
work.
